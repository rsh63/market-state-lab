## 4. Producer inflation firms ahead of the Fed decision

![Abstract U.S. producer inflation, Federal Reserve, and interest-rate-path graphic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-4-fed.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab Federal Reserve and economics graphic.*

**Fact:** The August Producer Price Index rose 0.4% month over month and 5.4% year over year. Energy prices increased 4.2%, while airline fares and hospital services—inputs to the Fed's preferred PCE measure—also rose. Rate futures moved to roughly a 70% probability of a quarter-point September increase. [Reuters, September 10](https://www.reuters.com/business/us-producer-prices-increase-expected-august-2026-09-10/)

**Inference:** The report strengthens the case for tightening, but its policy meaning depends on whether consumer inflation confirms broader persistence beyond the energy shock.

**Uncertainty:** Market probabilities are not FOMC commitments. CPI, revisions, the new PCE methodology, and geopolitical energy prices can change the interpretation.

**Why it matters:** Preserve separate hold and hike regimes. Model energy, core inflation, policy expectations, term premium, and Treasury supply independently rather than collapsing them into one rates signal.
