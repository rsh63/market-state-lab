## 1. TaskVM makes agent state and recovery explicit

![Three conceptual panels show a task contract, independent state verification, and classification of actions as reversible, compensatable or irreversible.](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a0/c4/a0c47efd-8ef4-418e-9889-d29466ec5731/content/images/2026/10/2026-10-16-story-1-taskvm-recovery.png)

*Graphic: Market State Lab. Source/date: Yang et al.; arXiv preprint submitted October 14, 2026. Research mechanisms and transaction panels are conceptual; quantitative charts use cited data.*

**Fact:** TaskVM represents an agent task as desired outcomes, facts to preserve and observed application state. It independently verifies outcomes and labels actions by recoverability. The authors report improved contract compliance and recovery control in their evaluations. [Source — Yang et al.; arXiv preprint submitted October 14, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20100v1)

**Inference:** Agent governance should specify state invariants and recovery options in addition to procedural plans.

**Uncertainty:** Version 1 is under refinement; interface-study results do not establish trading safety or financial performance.

**Why it matters:** For MATLAB-connected agents, define allowed state changes, preserve data and risk settings, verify outcomes independently, and require approval for irreversible actions.

## 2. A live-system case study finds six backtest/replay defects

![A conceptual map groups six reported defects into three data problems, two code problems and one configuration problem, with a no-profitability-claim caption.](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a0/c4/a0c47efd-8ef4-418e-9889-d29466ec5731/content/images/2026/10/2026-10-16-story-2-backtest-live-parity.png)

*Graphic: Market State Lab. Source/date: Vidovich and Zerjal; arXiv preprint submitted October 15, 2026. Research mechanisms and transaction panels are conceptual; quantitative charts use cited data.*

**Fact:** The authors report six defects in one intraday U.S. equity system: opening-volume feed coverage, dividend-adjusted historical prices, asymmetric downloads, sizing-limit mismatch, UTC/New York confusion, and settings not reaching order code. They say summary statistics did not expose the drift. [Source — Vidovich and Zerjal; arXiv preprint submitted October 15, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21846v1)

**Inference:** Agreement at the event and order level is a prerequisite for interpreting aggregate backtest results.

**Uncertainty:** This is one authors’ case study, not a prevalence estimate or independent profitability validation; the paper makes no profitability claim.

**Why it matters:** Replay the same timestamped inputs through research and paper-order paths, comparing quantities, prices, rejected orders and configuration fingerprints.

### DRL Improvements for the MATLAB System

1. Keep drawdown, concentration, turnover, liquidity and daily-loss limits outside the learned reward; verify constraints on the actual order path, with deterministic stop and recovery behavior.
2. Penalize implementation shortfall, inventory risk and excessive churn. Randomize spreads, depth withdrawal, latency, rejected cancels, partial fills and nonlinear impact.
3. Require event-level replay parity for timestamps, raw execution prices, sizing and configuration before evaluating a learned policy. Log observation, action, risk decision, order request and outcome separately.
4. Use purged, embargoed walk-forward folds and multiple seeds; hold out entire inflation and expiry blocks. Freeze selection rules, test regime robustness, and monitor calibration and drift.

### Model hygiene

Prevent leakage and look-ahead bias with decision-time timestamps and release vintages. Include delisted constituents to limit survivorship bias. Keep raw execution prices separate from adjusted research series; reconcile splits, distributions and other corporate actions. Reject stale options quotes, verify time zones and contract mappings, control multiple testing, and monitor calibration and feature/policy drift.

### MATLAB optimization patterns

- Align event, quote and order logs with time-zone-aware `timetable`, `retime` and `synchronize`; keep causal alignment explicit.
- Vectorize replay comparisons, preallocate diagnostic tables and profile environment/order steps before parallelizing.
- Run parallel `bayesopt` across independent purged folds with reproducible random streams and multiple seeds.
- Use GPUs for sufficiently large network batches; separate data, execution-cost, constraint, agent and logging modules.

### High-value quant research question

**Does enforcing feed, raw-price, time-zone, sizing and configuration parity reduce SPY/QQQ replay-to-paper-order divergence on monthly-expiry days?**

It is worth testing because event-level mismatch rates can expose implementation defects before aggregate returns obscure them; it does not require assuming predictive alpha.
