### Model hygiene

Prevent leakage, survivorship and look-ahead bias with point-in-time universes and first-known timestamps. Handle corporate actions consistently; reject stale options quotes; record rejected trials to control multiple testing. Calibrate out of sample and monitor feature, probability, policy, cost and regime drift.

### MATLAB optimization

- Align asynchronous market and macro data with `timetable`, `retime` and `synchronize`, preserving publication timestamps.
- Vectorize rolling features, preallocate buffers and profile the environment before parallelizing.
- Run parallel `bayesopt` across independent purged folds with reproducible random streams.
- Use GPUs for sufficiently large neural-network batches; keep environment, execution-cost, constraint and agent modules separate.
