### DRL Improvements for the MATLAB System

1. Enforce drawdown, concentration, turnover, liquidity and daily-loss constraints outside the learned reward; include implementation shortfall and downside-risk penalties inside it.
2. Add CPI phase, overnight/cash session, NQ–ES divergence, curve shift, quote age, auction state and expiry state; evaluate feature availability at decision time.
3. Randomize spread widening, depth withdrawal, latency jitter, rejected cancels, partial fills and nonlinear impact; require robust behavior across event and quiet regimes.
4. Use purged, embargoed walk-forward folds, frozen baselines and multiple seeds. Hold out entire inflation-release and expiry blocks and include realistic fees, spread and impact costs.

### Model hygiene

Preserve first-known timestamps; control leakage, survivorship and look-ahead bias. Reconcile splits, dividends and symbol changes before factor comparisons. Filter stale options quotes, retain unsuccessful trials, correct for multiple testing, calibrate out of sample and monitor feature, policy, probability and execution-cost drift.

### MATLAB optimization

- Align event, quote and session data with `timetable`, `retime` and `synchronize`; retain availability timestamps separately from event timestamps.
- Vectorize rolling features, preallocate replay buffers and profile environment steps before adding parallelism.
- Parallelize `bayesopt` only over independent purged folds; use reproducible random streams and multiple policy seeds.
- Use GPUs for sufficiently large neural-network batches. Keep market-session, execution-cost, risk-constraint and agent modules separate.
