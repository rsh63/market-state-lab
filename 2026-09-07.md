## 1. Cathay and Google expand AI-guided contrail avoidance

![Abstract aviation-AI, forecasting, and climate-optimization graphic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-1-ai.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab AI graphic.*

**Fact:** Cathay Pacific and Google expanded trials of an AI-based system that forecasts where climate-warming contrails are likely to form so pilots can adjust altitude. The initial test covered more than 80 flights and produced an estimated 40% reduction in contrail warming impact; a larger second phase will cover Asian and trans-Pacific routes. [Reuters, September 7](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/cathay-pacific-google-expand-ai-trials-cut-climate-warming-aircraft-contrails-2026-09-07/)

**Inference:** This is a practical example of machine learning supporting constrained real-time decisions rather than replacing the human operator.

**Uncertainty:** The estimate is company-reported. Fuel burn, routing constraints, forecast error, operational reliability, and independently measured climate benefits remain to be established at scale.

**Why it matters:** Use the same pattern for MATLAB agents: probabilistic forecasts, explicit constraints, operator approval, and counterfactual logging. Optimize total system cost—not a single prediction metric.

## 2. Public data cannot uniquely reconstruct institutional metaorders

![Abstract metaorder, market-impact, and quantitative-finance graphic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-2-quant.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab quantitative-finance graphic.*

**Fact:** A quantitative-finance paper revised September 3 tested synthetic metaorder reconstruction using public transaction and quote data for 239 Johannesburg Stock Exchange names from 2023–2025. Parameter choices could reproduce aggregate impact patterns or the Lillo–Mike–Farmer order-splitting relation, but doing one did not reliably validate the other. [Goliath and Gebbie, arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19590)

**Inference:** Anonymous public data can support useful impact proxies, but reconstructed parent orders are model-dependent latent variables—not observed ground truth.

**Uncertainty:** The study covers one exchange, one reconstruction family, and historical data. Results may differ across venues, asset classes, and identifier-rich proprietary datasets.

**Why it matters:** Validate execution models on multiple stylized facts and held-out symbols. Propagate reconstruction uncertainty into slippage estimates instead of treating inferred metaorders as labels.
