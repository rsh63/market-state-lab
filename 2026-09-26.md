## 2. Kalshi ruling highlights jurisdiction risk in event markets

*Credit: Market State Lab, existing quantitative-finance category illustration.*

**Fact:** The Sixth Circuit ruled that Ohio and Tennessee can regulate Kalshi event contracts under state gambling laws, deepening a federal circuit split. Kalshi expects further legal review. [Reuters, September 25](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/us-appeals-court-rules-against-kalshi-says-states-can-regulate-prediction-2026-09-25/)

**Inference:** Contract access and legal status belong in market-data provenance and structural-break models.

### Model hygiene

Prevent leakage, survivorship bias, and look-ahead bias with point-in-time universes, first-known timestamps, and corporate-action handling. Preserve session boundaries and stale-options flags. Record rejected trials to control multiple testing; calibrate out of sample and monitor feature, probability, policy, cost, and regime drift.

### MATLAB optimization

- Align market, macro, auction, and expiry data with `timetable`, `retime`, and `synchronize`; avoid forward-looking interpolation.
- Vectorize rolling features, preallocate episode buffers, and profile bottlenecks before parallelizing.
- Run parallel `bayesopt` across independent purged folds with reproducible random streams; retain an untouched final evaluation block.
- Use GPUs for sufficiently large neural-network batches; keep environment, costs, constraints, and agent modules separate.
