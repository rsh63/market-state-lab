## 1. AI trust becomes a measurable adoption constraint

![Abstract AI governance, human oversight, and public-trust illustration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-1-ai.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab category illustration; not an event photograph.*

**Fact:** A Reuters/Ipsos poll published October 7 found that 57% of registered voters thought the administration was not taking AI risks seriously enough or at all; 54% said the same of Congress. The online survey ended October 5 and included 3,526 registered voters among 4,506 adults, with a reported two-point margin of error. [Reuters/Ipsos, October 7](https://www.reuters.com/world/most-us-voters-say-trump-congress-dont-take-ai-risks-seriously-reutersipsos-poll-2026-10-07/)

**Inference:** Public acceptance could influence enterprise deployment, permitting, and oversight requirements.

**Uncertainty:** Attitudes do not establish future legislation, actual adoption, or technical failure rates.

**Why it matters:** Evaluate AI agents on auditability, permission boundaries, incident detection, and safe fallback alongside model performance.

## 2. India may reverse part of its derivatives settlement overhaul

![Abstract derivatives settlement, closing auctions, and execution-model illustration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-2-quant.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab category illustration; not an event photograph.*

**Fact:** Reuters reported October 5, citing two sources, that SEBI is considering suspending closing auctions for derivatives settlement for at least a year and returning to a final-30-minute volume-weighted average. The auction system introduced in August had produced sharp expiry-day swings. [Reuters, October 5](https://www.reuters.com/world/india-markets-regulator-partly-reverse-derivative-settlement-rules-after-2026-10-05/)

**Inference:** Market-design changes can invalidate calibrated closing-impact and settlement-basis models.

**Uncertainty:** This is a reported proposal, not a final rule; SEBI did not respond to the cited request for comment.

**Why it matters:** Version settlement rules and segment pre-change and post-change samples. Stress auction concentration, basis risk, fill probability, and hedging costs.

### DRL Improvements for the MATLAB System

1. Add auction/minutes phase, first-known policy-text features, NQ–ES strength, curve shifts, VX slope, quote age, depth, and settlement-rule version.
2. Enforce drawdown, concentration, turnover, liquidity, and daily-loss limits outside the reward; penalize downside risk, missed fills, and implementation shortfall within it.
3. Randomize auction surprises, yield/oil reversals, depth collapse, spread widening, latency, stale quotes, partial fills, and nonlinear impact.
4. Use purged, embargoed walk-forward folds, frozen baselines, multiple seeds, and untouched policy-event and market-design-change blocks. Compare performance after realistic costs and under constrained risk budgets.

### Model hygiene

Control leakage, survivorship and look-ahead bias, first-known timestamps, corporate actions, stale options quotes, and multiple testing. Separate sessions and settlement-rule versions; retain rejected trials, calibrate out of sample, and monitor feature, probability, policy, cost, and regime drift. Never use post-release text to predict a pre-release trade.

### MATLAB optimization

- Align policy, auction, quote, and expiry data with `timetable`, `retime`, and `synchronize`; retain release timestamps and quote age.
- Vectorize rolling features, preallocate episode buffers, and profile before parallelizing.
- Run parallel `bayesopt` across independent purged folds with reproducible random streams.
- Use GPUs for sufficiently large neural-network batches; keep environment, execution-cost, constraint, and agent modules separate.

### High-value research question

**Does the interaction of pre-minutes NQ–ES relative strength, the ten-year auction yield surprise, and the two-/ten-year yield response predict QQQ continuation versus reversal after the minutes, net of realistic spread, impact, and 0DTE hedging costs?**

This tests whether auction information adds value beyond the policy-text and yield response. Define the trading decision time after all included features become available; compare against a yield-only baseline.
