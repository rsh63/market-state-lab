## 2. Earnings-call research exposes within-event timing leakage

![A conceptual availability contract links input boundaries, evidence of completion plus processing time, and an outcome window that starts after usable input; the authors’ baseline changed 9.4% of return signs.](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a0/c4/a0c47efd-8ef4-418e-9889-d29466ec5731/content/images/2026/10/2026-10-17-story-2-earnings-availability.png)

*Graphic: Market State Lab; Ma et al., arXiv preprint 2610.21659v1, submitted October 15, 2026. Conceptual sequence, not a calibrated timeline.*

**Fact:** In an October 15 arXiv preprint, Ma and coauthors examine 552 earnings calls from 24 firms. Moving the outcome start from a call-start proxy to a completion proxy changes 9.4% of return signs in their baseline, with the return endpoint held fixed. Their Availability Contract makes input and outcome clocks explicit. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21659v1)

**Inference:** Chronological train/test splits alone cannot remove leakage when a target begins before an event’s model input is complete.

**Uncertainty:** Completion is reconstructed from audio plus a stipulated processing allowance; timing evidence and benchmark design limit generalization.

**Why it matters:** Timestamp transcript/audio completion, processing and order eligibility separately; compare event labels under alternative availability allowances before retraining.

### DRL Improvements for the MATLAB System

1. Add an information-availability gate: a feature becomes observable only after its full input and processing latency are complete. Test multiple plausible completion allowances without moving the outcome endpoint opportunistically.
2. Enforce drawdown, exposure, concentration and liquidity limits outside the learned reward. Reconcile expiry-generated positions before the policy can submit new orders; preserve deterministic stop and recovery behavior.
3. Reward net implementation performance after spread, impact, fees and inventory risk. Randomize quote withdrawal, partial fills, failed cancels and latency; include a penalty for unnecessary turnover.
4. Use purged, embargoed walk-forward folds, multiple seeds and held-out volatility/expiry regimes. Freeze model-selection rules and report both pathwise risk breaches and calibration/drift diagnostics.

### Model hygiene

Prevent within-event leakage as well as cross-sample look-ahead: retain input-completion, release-vintage and decision timestamps. Use point-in-time universes including delisted securities to limit survivorship bias. Reconcile splits and distributions while separating adjusted research prices from executable raw prices. Reject stale options quotes, verify contract identifiers and time zones, control multiple testing, and monitor calibration and feature/policy drift.

### MATLAB optimization patterns

- Use time-zone-aware `timetable` logs and causal `synchronize`/`retime` rules; assert that feature-availability timestamps precede decisions.
- Vectorize event-clock and order-log comparisons, preallocate diagnostics, and profile the environment before adding workers.
- Parallelize independent walk-forward folds or `bayesopt` trials with reproducible random streams; keep holdout selection frozen.
- Use GPUs for large neural-network batches when profiling supports them; separate data, availability, execution-cost, risk and agent modules.
