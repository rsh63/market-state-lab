## 2. Quote-feed construction can masquerade as self-excitation

![Two evidence panels: Raw NBBO: fleeting artifacts; Persisting mid-price: cleaner events. Source and date captioned; conceptual summary, not a calibrated model.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/227cb84c49f228db5745e6e114ebf431442ef7cc/2026-10-29-story-2-quote-feed-artifacts.png)

*Graphic: Market State Lab; Lee, arXiv:2610.36317v1; October 26, 2026. Evidence summary or quantitative chart as labeled; no simulated curves.*

**Date:** Submitted October 26; listed October 28.

**Fact:** A preprint finds that fleeting locks and depleted quotes create apparent sub-millisecond Hawkes excitation in large-tick stocks. Requiring a mid-price level to persist one millisecond removes that component. [Direct source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36317)

**Inference:** Event definitions can change measured clustering before a strategy sees any true signal.

**Uncertainty:** The result is feed- and stock-dependent; persistence filtering changes the event target and can introduce delay.

**Why it matters:** Compare raw and causally confirmed events with identical windows and realistic latency.

### DRL Improvements for the MATLAB System

1. Enforce drawdown, exposure, participation and liquidity constraints outside the learned policy; preserve a safe fallback for stale data and unfamiliar states.
2. Penalize turnover, spread, impact, tail losses and missed fills in the reward; compare wealth and inventory-penalized utility separately.
3. Define quote events causally; confirm persistence only after the required observation interval, and include the confirmation latency.
4. Use purged, embargoed walk-forward folds, matched seeds and held-out regimes; stress event-time gaps, cancellations, latency and spread widening against multiple fixed-spread baselines.

### Model hygiene

Use point-in-time universes and availability timestamps; prevent leakage, survivorship and look-ahead bias. Reconcile corporate actions causally and preserve adjusted/unadjusted basis. Reject stale options and expired contracts. Control multiple testing, test calibration, and monitor feature, cost and policy drift. Preserve unrevised economic vintages and delayed financial labels.

### MATLAB optimization patterns

- Use timezone-aware timetables with causal synchronize/retime alignment.
- Vectorize features, preallocate logs and profile before parallelization.
- Parallelize independent walk-forward folds or Bayesian optimization trials with reproducible random streams and immutable holdouts.
- Use GPUs for sufficiently large training batches; keep environment, risk and execution modules modular.

### High-value quant research question

**Does causal quote-persistence filtering improve out-of-sample execution shortfall after accounting for confirmation latency?**

Run paired, purged walk-forward tests with identical order flow and risk limits. This tests whether removing feed artifacts adds value after the delay it creates.
