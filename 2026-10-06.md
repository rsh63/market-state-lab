*Credit: original Market State Lab category illustration; not an event photograph.*

**Fact:** OpenAI and Anthropic told an Australian parliamentary hearing on October 6 that they would welcome laws requiring disclosure of data breaches carried out by their AI agents. [Reuters, October 6](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/australias-abc-rejects-ai-copyright-carveout-believes-already-been-scraped-2026-10-06/)

## 4. Services growth slows while input-price pressure increases

![Abstract U.S. services activity, input-price pressure, and monetary-policy illustration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-4-fed.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab category illustration; not an event photograph.*

**Fact:** ISM’s October 5 release put September services PMI at 54.9, down from 55.4. Its prices index rose to 74.0 from 72.6, the highest since July 2022; employment edged into expansion at 50.1. **Why it matters:** Keep demand, employment, and price components separate. Preserve first-release timestamps and test their incremental value beyond yields and market prices.

- Separate overnight, premarket, the 8:30 a.m. CT opening auction, first 30 minutes, and closing flows. Check the release and speaker calendar before routing.

4. Use purged, embargoed walk-forward folds, multiple seeds, frozen baselines, and untouched policy and thin-liquidity regimes. Charge for missed fills as well as completed trades.

### Model hygiene

Control leakage, survivorship and look-ahead bias, first-known timestamps, corporate actions, stale options quotes, and multiple testing. Separate sessions and venues, retain rejected trials, calibrate out of sample, and monitor feature, probability, policy, regime, and transaction-cost drift.

### MATLAB optimization

- Align macro, quotes, sessions, and expiries with `timetable`, `retime`, and `synchronize`, retaining release times and quote age.
- Vectorize rolling features, preallocate buffers, and profile environment steps before parallelizing.
- Run parallel `bayesopt` across independent purged folds with reproducible random streams.
- Reserve GPUs for large neural-network batches; separate environment, cost, constraint, and agent modules.
