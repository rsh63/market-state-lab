## 2. Equity-linked prediction markets raise market-structure questions

![Abstract equity prediction contracts, settlement, and quantitative-finance illustration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-2-quant.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab category illustration; not an event photograph.*

**Fact:** Allium analysis reported by Reuters found more than $220 million wagered across approximately 31,000 equity-linked Polymarket International markets through early September. Roughly 60% concerned individual stocks. [Reuters, September 28](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/prediction-markets-push-into-us-stocks-raises-regulatory-alarm-bells-2026-09-28/)

**Inference:** These contracts may supply alternative signals, but their market structure differs from equities.

**Uncertainty:** Reported turnover does not establish executable depth or signal quality; regulatory treatment remains unsettled.

**Why it matters:** Validate payoff definitions, settlement references, fees, access, and quote freshness. Binary probabilities are not expected stock returns.

- Separate overnight futures, premarket ETFs, the opening auction, first 30 minutes, quarter-end flows, and the September 30 BEA release.

- Reject stale, crossed, zero-bid, one-sided, or thin options quotes. Refresh surfaces after major oil or yield moves.

### Model hygiene

Control leakage, survivorship and look-ahead bias, first-known timestamps, revised macro vintages, corporate actions, stale options quotes, and multiple testing. Calibrate out of sample, retain rejected trials, and monitor feature, probability, policy, transaction-cost, and regime drift.

### MATLAB optimization

- Align market and macro publication times with `timetable`, `retime`, and `synchronize`; preserve original observation and release timestamps.
- Vectorize rolling features, preallocate episode buffers, and profile environment steps before parallelizing.
- Run parallel `bayesopt` across independent purged folds with reproducible random streams and multiple seeds.
- Reserve GPUs for sufficiently large neural-network batches; keep environment, execution-cost, constraint, session, and agent modules separate.
