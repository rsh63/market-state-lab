## 2. Future-trained forecasting models do not automatically outperform

![Abstract point-in-time forecasting, model vintages, and quantitative-finance graphic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-2-quant.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab quantitative-finance graphic.*

**Fact:** A September 17 preprint compares time-series foundation-model vintages across 14 equity markets and four horizons. Future-trained vintages produced higher mean-squared error in 18 of 20 U.S. model-set/horizon combinations; global and factor-augmented results were mixed. [Chen et al., arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20554)

**Inference:** Look-ahead contamination invalidates the information set, but does not necessarily inflate measured performance.

**Uncertainty:** These setting-specific preprint results do not show that leakage is harmless or universally reduces accuracy.

**Why it matters:** Freeze model and data vintages. Audit information availability separately from out-of-sample accuracy and net economic value.

### DRL Improvements for the MATLAB System

1. Record model-training cutoffs, first-known headline timestamps, session labels, gap state, NQ–ES strength, and quote age.
2. Enforce drawdown, concentration, turnover, liquidity, and daily-loss constraints outside the reward; penalize downside risk and implementation shortfall inside it.
3. Randomize opening gaps, depth collapses, partial fills, stale quotes, latency, policy shocks, and nonlinear impact.
4. Use purged, embargoed walk-forward tests, frozen point-in-time baselines, multiple seeds, regime scorecards, and untouched event blocks.

### Model hygiene

Audit leakage, survivorship and look-ahead bias, model vintages, first-known timestamps, corporate actions and distributions, stale options quotes, and multiple testing. Calibrate out of sample and monitor feature, probability, policy, cost, and regime drift.

### MATLAB optimization

- Align market, macro, model-vintage, auction, and expiry data with `timetable`, `retime`, and `synchronize`.
- Vectorize rolling features, preallocate episode buffers, and profile before parallelizing.
- Run parallel `bayesopt` across independent purged folds using reproducible random streams.
- Reserve GPUs for sufficiently large batches; keep environment, cost, risk constraints, and agent modules separate.
