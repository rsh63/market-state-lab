## 2. Cboe C2 launches a 10-millisecond Step-Up options auction

![Abstract options-auction, latency, and quantitative-finance graphic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-2-quant.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab quantitative-finance graphic.*

**Fact:** Effective September 4, subject to regulatory review, Cboe C2 Options introduced its system-generated Step-Up Mechanism. It can expose a marketable order when C2 is not at the NBBO before routing, canceling, or booking it; the maximum exposure period is 10 milliseconds across C1, EDGX, and C2. [Cboe notice](https://www.cboe.com/notices/content/?id=60729)

**Inference:** The mechanism may improve price opportunity while changing routing latency, queue priority, fill probability, and adverse selection.

**Uncertainty:** Realized price improvement, response concentration, cancel rates, and latency costs are not yet established. Rule approval and participant configuration matter.

**Why it matters:** Tag SUM executions separately, model the 10-millisecond exposure plus matching-engine buffer, and compare price improvement against opportunity cost and information leakage. Do not pool auction fills with ordinary routed orders.
