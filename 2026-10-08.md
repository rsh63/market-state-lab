*Credit: original Market State Lab category illustration; not an event photograph.*

**Fact:** In an October 8 speech, Governor Christopher Waller said he anticipates additional rate increases if data evolve as expected, but increases need not occur at consecutive meetings. He described inflation as too high and the labor market as stable. [Federal Reserve, October 8](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/waller20261008a.htm).

### DRL Improvements for the MATLAB System

1. Add session, claims/auction phase, oil and yield changes, NQ–ES strength, factor dispersion, quote age, depth, and expiry state using first-known timestamps.
2. Enforce drawdown, concentration, turnover, liquidity, and daily-loss limits outside the reward; penalize downside risk, implementation shortfall, and non-executable fills within it.
3. Randomize oil/yield shocks, depth withdrawal, latency, spread widening, partial fills, and nonlinear impact; include abstention when market data are unreliable.
4. Use purged, embargoed walk-forward evaluation with frozen baselines, multiple seeds, regime scorecards, and untouched event blocks. Report capital-preservation failures as well as average performance.

### Model hygiene

Prevent leakage, survivorship and look-ahead bias; retain first-known timestamps, corporate actions, delistings, and historical index membership. Reject stale options quotes, track multiple testing and rejected trials, calibrate out of sample, and monitor feature, policy, probability, execution-cost, and regime drift. Freeze AI model versions and evaluation prompts before comparisons.

### MATLAB optimization

- Align quote, macro, factor, and expiry data with `timetable`, `retime`, and `synchronize`; preserve quote age and release time.
- Vectorize rolling features, preallocate buffers, and profile environment steps before parallelizing.
- Parallelize independent purged folds or `bayesopt` evaluations with reproducible random streams; keep the holdout untouched.
- Use GPUs for sufficiently large neural-network batches; keep data, environment, costs, risk constraints, and agents in separate modules.
