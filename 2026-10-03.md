## 2. SEC approves listing rules for six 3x commodity and crypto products

![Abstract leveraged exchange-traded products, daily rebalancing, and quantitative-risk illustration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-2-quant.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab category illustration; not an event photograph.*

**Fact:** An October 2 SEC order approved Cboe BZX’s proposal to list six VS Trust products covering gold, silver, bitcoin, ether, crude oil, and natural gas. Each seeks three times its benchmark’s daily performance before fees and expenses. [SEC order 34-106577, October 2](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/cboebzx/2026/34-106577.pdf)

**Inference:** Daily leverage makes compounding, rebalance timing, funding, and underlying-market hours central modeling variables.

**Uncertainty:** Listing-rule approval does not establish a launch date, executable depth, tight spreads, or a three-times return over longer holding periods.

**Why it matters:** Simulate daily resets and path dependence; include financing, roll, basis, and closing-flow costs. Validate the final prospectus and observed liquidity before adding a product to a strategy universe.

## 4. September payrolls slow; earlier employment gains are revised lower

![Abstract U.S. employment revisions, wage growth, and Federal Reserve policy illustration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-4-fed.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab category illustration; not an event photograph.*

**Fact:** BLS reported October 2 that September payrolls increased 29,000 and unemployment was 4.2%. Hourly earnings rose 0.1% monthly and 3.0% annually. July and August payrolls were revised down by a combined 60,000. [BLS, October 2](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm)

**Inference:** The labor signal is softer than the earlier data vintage suggested, but policy also depends on inflation and financial conditions.

**Uncertainty:** Initial payroll estimates are revised and subject to sampling and seasonal-adjustment uncertainty; the two surveys measure different concepts.

**Why it matters:** Store first-release and revised vintages separately. Model jobs, wages, participation, revisions, and the yield response as distinct features rather than a single bullish or bearish label.
