# Risk disclosure

Market State Lab provides impersonal research and educational material. It is not a registered investment adviser, broker-dealer, commodity trading adviser, or fiduciary, and it does not provide individualized investment, legal, tax, or accounting advice.

Nothing published is an offer, solicitation, recommendation, or guarantee. Securities, derivatives, futures, options, leverage, and algorithmic systems involve substantial risk, including loss of principal. Options can expire worthless, and short options can create losses beyond the premium received. Model outputs can fail because of regime change, bad data, latency, crowding, implementation error, or assumptions that do not survive live trading.

Past or simulated performance does not predict future results. Backtests are especially sensitive to look-ahead bias, survivorship bias, data revisions, corporate actions, stale quotes, transaction costs, and multiple testing. Readers are responsible for independent verification and their own decisions.
