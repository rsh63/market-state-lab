# Market State Lab

**Five verified market signals—plus volatility, execution-risk, factor, and MATLAB-system implications before the U.S. open.**

Market State Lab publishes the free **Quant Market Brief** for quantitative traders, MATLAB users, and market practitioners. Each edition separates facts, inference, and uncertainty across exactly five developments, then translates them into a market-state dashboard, execution controls, model-hygiene reminders, and one testable research question.

## Reproducibility and disclosure

- Facts link to primary sources or high-quality reporting.
- Inference and uncertainty are labeled explicitly.
- Market readings identify whether they are live, delayed, indicative, or prior-close when known.
- Research ideas must be tested with point-in-time data, realistic costs, purged walk-forward evaluation, and independent risk controls.

Editorial content is © Market State Lab. Code in `marketStateScore.m` is released under the MIT License. Third-party facts and data remain subject to their providers' terms.

This publication is for research and education—not individualized financial advice. Markets involve risk, and models can fail.
