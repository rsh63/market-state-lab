## 2. Singapore's U.S.-stock receipts illustrate a liquidity gap

![Abstract cross-market trading, liquidity, and quantitative-finance graphic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-2-quant.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab quantitative-finance category graphic; illustrative.*

**Fact:** Reuters reported September 23 that SpaceX's Singapore Depository Receipts traded about $2.3 million from their July launch through September 18, versus $336.6 billion for the U.S. shares over the same period. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/singapores-link-us-stocks-is-struggling-attract-traders-2026-09-23/)

**Inference:** Access to an underlying stock does not automatically reproduce its liquidity in another wrapper or time zone.

**Why it matters:** Model venue, session, currency conversion, creation/redemption constraints, basis, and capacity separately. Require executable quotes rather than importing the underlying market's cost assumptions.

## 4. Williams defends the Fed's rate-control framework

![Abstract Federal Reserve operations, repo markets, and Treasury liquidity graphic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-4-fed.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab Federal Reserve and economics category graphic; illustrative.*

**Fact:** In September 22 remarks, New York Fed President John Williams said ample reserves, overnight reverse repos, and standing repo operations have supported interest-rate control. He emphasized adapting implementation tools as market structure and reserve demand change. [New York Fed speech](https://www.newyorkfed.org/newsevents/speeches/2026/wil260922)

**Inference:** Policy restrictiveness and the plumbing that transmits policy are separate state variables.

**Uncertainty:** An effective framework does not rule out localized funding stress; these remarks do not establish the next policy-rate decision.

**Why it matters:** Track secured and unsecured funding, reserve demand, collateral, and Treasury liquidity separately from directional rate forecasts. Do not interpret a liquidity backstop as an easing signal.

### Execution implications

- Separate overnight, premarket, the 8:30 a.m. CT cash opening auction, first 30 minutes, and closing auction. Recheck scheduled PMI/Fed-speaker times before trading; exact event times were not independently verified here.
- Use auction-aware limits, participation caps, fresh spread/depth/quote-age gates, and realized-slippage kill switches. No fresh two-sided market means no modeled executable fill.
- Rebuild SPY/QQQ option surfaces and strike-level liquidity after opening. Same-day expiries and the September 23 weekly VX expiry can concentrate hedging, but dealer gamma direction and strike concentration were not verified. Do not confuse weekly VX settlement with monthly expiry. [Cboe contract dates](https://www.cboe.com/markets/us/futures/market-statistics/settlement/futures/daily/)

### DRL Improvements for the MATLAB System

1. Add venue/session, quote age, spread, depth, auction state, expiry state, oil return, NQ–ES relative strength, and funding-state features; enforce an explicit missing-data state.
2. Keep drawdown, concentration, turnover, liquidity, and daily-loss constraints outside the learned reward. Penalize downside risk, implementation shortfall, and non-executable fills inside it.
3. Randomize spread widening, depth withdrawal, partial fills, latency, stale quotes, FX basis, and nonlinear impact; evaluate no-trade behavior during liquidity gaps.
4. Use purged, embargoed walk-forward evaluation, frozen baselines, multiple seeds, and untouched event/session blocks. Report net performance and constraint breaches by regime.

### Model hygiene

Control leakage, survivorship and look-ahead bias, first-known timestamps, corporate actions, stale options quotes, and multiple testing. Separate Close from Adjusted Close; align releases by availability, not observation date. Calibrate out of sample, retain rejected trials, and monitor feature, probability, policy, cost, and regime drift.

### MATLAB optimization

- Use `timetable`, `retime`, and `synchronize` with explicit session/time-zone and staleness rules; do not forward-fill across closed markets by default.
- Vectorize rolling features, preallocate episode buffers, and profile environment steps before parallelizing.
- Run parallel `bayesopt` over independent purged evaluations with reproducible random streams and multiple policy seeds.
- Use GPUs only for suitable supported workloads with enough batch size; separate market data, execution cost, constraints, and agent modules.

### High-value research question

**Does adding session-specific spread, depth, and quote-age gates improve the net out-of-sample performance of a QQQ–SPY relative-strength opening strategy versus using price signals alone?**

Worth testing because venue access and calm index prices can disguise non-executable opportunities. Compare matched signals, opportunity cost, adverse selection, tail slippage, and rejected trades—not only the Sharpe ratio of filled orders.
