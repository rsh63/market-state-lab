## 2. Fed research finds futures curves are useful—but not unbiased forecasts

![Abstract commodity-futures curve, forecasting, and quantitative-finance graphic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-2-quant.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab quantitative-finance graphic.*

**Fact:** A September 3 Federal Reserve note tested 12 commodities using monthly data beginning as early as 2005. Futures generally beat random-walk, momentum, and mean-reversion benchmarks when the curve differed materially from spot, but the authors found that futures prices were not generally unbiased forecasts and that absolute errors grew when curve slopes were extreme. [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/revisiting-the-forecasting-potential-of-futures-prices-20260903.html)

**Inference:** Curve shape is informative as a conditional feature, but it also embeds hedging pressure, storage economics, convenience yield, and liquidity—not only expectations.

**Uncertainty:** Results vary by commodity and maturity, and historical relative RMSE does not establish live tradability or stability during a new supply shock.

**Why it matters:** Compare curve signals against naive baselines by commodity and regime. Model inventories, carry, liquidity, and hedging demand explicitly; calibrate probabilistic forecasts instead of treating futures as point predictions.

## 3. U.S. equity funds post a second weekly outflow

![Abstract U.S. equity-fund flows, SPY, QQQ, and market-liquidity graphic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-3-markets.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab U.S. markets graphic.*

**Fact:** LSEG Lipper data showed investors withdrew a net $11.12 billion from U.S. equity funds in the week through September 2, after $22.72 billion of outflows the prior week. Large-cap funds lost $7.52 billion; technology funds lost $1.39 billion; money-market funds gained $48.76 billion. [Reuters, September 4](https://www.reuters.com/business/us-equity-funds-record-second-weekly-outflow-iran-tensions-high-yields-2026-09-04/)

**Inference:** Investors are raising liquidity while remaining selective rather than executing a uniform risk exit; Friday's relative QQQ resilience also shows that flow and price signals can diverge.

**Uncertainty:** Weekly fund-flow data are aggregated and lagged. They do not reveal intraday timing, derivatives hedges, investor type, or whether the withdrawals will persist.

**Why it matters:** Treat fund flows as a slow state variable, not an entry signal. Confirm with breadth, NQ–ES relative strength, Treasury yields, auction conditions, and realized execution costs after the holiday.

**Fact:** August PPI is due September 10 and CPI September 11, ahead of the September 15–16 FOMC meeting. A Reuters poll expected headline CPI to rise 0.4% month over month and core CPI 0.2%; late-Friday fed-funds futures implied a 57% probability of a rate increase. [Reuters, September 4](https://www.reuters.com/business/wall-st-week-ahead-investors-pore-over-inflation-data-signals-rate-trajectory-2026-09-04/)

**Inference:** After stronger payrolls, inflation data now have unusually high leverage over the near-term policy distribution and front-end yields.
