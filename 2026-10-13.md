## 2. Causal market-impact research challenges flow-price regressions

![Conceptual comparison of observed trading flow and information with the counterfactual price with versus without an order](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a0/c4/a0c47efd-8ef4-418e-9889-d29466ec5731/content/images/2026/10/2026-10-13-story-2-causal-impact.png)

*Graphic: Market State Lab. Conceptual mechanism; Thumm and Chen, October 8 preprint.*

**Fact:** An October 8 preprint trains a causal prior-data fitted network on synthetic markets with hidden informational flow. It tests intervention responses in an agent-based order book and real market events. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.12537v1)

**Inference:** Price movement associated with an order can combine trading impact and information; treating the entire movement as impact can misprice execution costs.

**Uncertainty:** Identification comes from the prior. The training market lacks queueing, cancellations and inventory; real-market causal transfer is not proven. Mislabeled events can produce confident false impact estimates, and interval calibration does not survive transfer automatically.

**Why it matters:** Compare conventional cost models with a counterfactual simulator, using replay controls, placebo events and held-out calibration. Do not replace production impact estimates based on this proof of concept alone.

### DRL Improvements for the MATLAB System

1. Compare fixed, spread-based and causal impact models under identical episodes and exposure; retain a frozen execution baseline.
2. Enforce drawdown, exposure, turnover, liquidity and loss constraints outside the reward. Penalize implementation shortfall, adverse selection and infeasible fills.
3. Randomize depth, quote failures, partial fills, latency and sector gaps; stress mislabeled events and synthetic-prior mismatch.
4. Use purged, embargoed walk-forward evaluation, multiple seeds and untouched earnings/inflation blocks. Recalibrate cost intervals on held-out data and monitor their coverage.

### Model hygiene

Control leakage, look-ahead and survivorship bias with first-known timestamps, label-horizon purging and point-in-time constituents. Reconcile corporate actions and dividend conventions. Audit stale options quotes and event labels, account for multiple testing, retain failed trials, calibrate out of sample, and monitor feature, policy, cost and regime drift.

### MATLAB optimization

- Align market sessions, speeches and releases using `timetable`, `retime` and `synchronize`, preserving timezone and quote-age metadata.
- Preallocate buffers and vectorize rolling features; profile simulation and environment steps before parallelizing.
- Run parallel `bayesopt` across independent purged folds with reproducible random streams; avoid nested oversubscription.
- Use GPUs when workload size justifies transfer overhead; keep data, execution-cost, environment and risk modules separate.
