## 1. NVIDIA doubles entry-level edge-AI inference with Jetson Orin Nano 2

![Abstract neural-network and edge-AI hardware graphic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-1-ai.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab AI graphic.*

**Fact:** NVIDIA announced Jetson Orin Nano 2 on August 25 with 78 trillion operations per second, 8GB of memory, and an eight-core Arm CPU. NVIDIA says it provides twice the inference performance of its predecessor in the same form factor and uses 40% less power at equivalent performance in 15-watt mode. Availability is expected in the first half of 2027. [NVIDIA announcement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-jetson-orin-nano-2-robotics-computer-to-redefine-entry-level-edge-ai)

**Inference:** Smaller vision-language and reasoning models are making local inference more practical for latency-sensitive robotics and industrial systems.

**Uncertainty:** Performance and efficiency claims are vendor-reported; application-level latency, thermal behavior, reliability, pricing, and production availability remain unverified.

**Why it matters:** For MATLAB deployment, benchmark generated code and exported networks on end-to-end task latency, memory, power, numerical equivalence, and failure recovery—not headline operations per second alone.

*Credit: original Market State Lab quantitative-finance graphic.*

Initial weekly expiries are September 4 and September 11. [HKEX, August 28](https://www.hkex.com.hk/News/News-Release/2026/260828news?sc_lang=en)

**Why it matters:** Build IPO-specific cost models. Do not infer liquidity from contract availability; require observed quotes, depth, borrow, auction participation, and fill quality before using SHN options in systematic strategies.
