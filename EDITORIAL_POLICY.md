# Editorial, corrections, and conflicts policy

## Independence

Market State Lab does not accept payment for favorable coverage. Sponsorship, affiliate relationships, data-vendor relationships, and material positions must be disclosed in the relevant issue.

## Corrections

Material factual errors are corrected in place with a timestamped note describing what changed. Silent edits are limited to spelling, formatting, and broken links. The GitHub history acts as an additional public audit trail.

## Forecast accountability

The publication does not convert commentary into an implied track record. A directional or model claim counts as a forecast only if it is timestamped, has a defined horizon and evaluation rule, and remains visible after the outcome.

## Images

Prefer original data graphics, public-domain government imagery, or images with explicit reuse permission. Every third-party image requires a credit and source. Never assume that embedding a publisher's photo is licensed for redistribution.
