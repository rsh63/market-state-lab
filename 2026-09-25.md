## 2. Cboe’s auction-duration trial changes the execution clock

![Abstract quantitative-finance and electronic-market category illustration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-2-quant.png)

*Credit: Market State Lab original quantitative-finance category illustration.*

**Fact:** Cboe scheduled a minimum-duration trial starting September 21 for Danish, Finnish, and Swedish securities on its DXE Periodic Auction Book, ending November 30. Auctions cannot finish before the minimum period; the 100-millisecond maximum and allocation rules are unchanged. [Cboe notice](https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/release_notes/2026/Cboe-Europe-Equities-Announces-PAB-Minimum-Auction-Duration.pdf)

**Inference:** Changing the distribution of auction end times may alter participation, fill probability, and adverse selection even without changing a strategy’s signal.

**Uncertainty:** Cboe does not disclose the minimum duration or its algorithm. Realized execution effects have not been established; this is a current-week implementation notice, not a new announcement today.

**Why it matters:** Compare affected securities with matched controls, preserving auction phase, order arrival, cancellation, fill, and post-fill price data. Do not transfer European trial results directly to U.S. opening auctions.

## 4. Initial jobless claims remain low; continuing claims edge up

*Credit: Market State Lab original economics category illustration.*

**Fact:** The September 24 Labor Department release put initial claims at 197,000 for the week ended September 19, down 1,000 from a revised prior week. The four-week average fell to 202,250. Continuing claims rose 2,000 to 1,719,000 for the week ended September 12. [U.S. Department of Labor](https://www.dol.gov/ui/data.pdf)

**Inference:** Layoff filings do not yet signal a sharp deterioration, but low initial claims alone do not demonstrate strong hiring.

- Refresh spreads, depth, quote age, auction imbalances, and option surfaces after the release and open. Use auction-aware limits, participation caps, and realized-slippage kill switches.

### DRL Improvements for the MATLAB System

1. Include event countdown, market-session and auction phase, quote age, spread/depth state, NQ–ES strength, curve changes, and expiry state. Keep missing-data flags explicit.
2. Enforce drawdown, concentration, turnover, liquidity, and daily-loss limits outside the learned reward; penalize downside risk, non-executable fills, and implementation shortfall within it.
3. Randomize auction timing, latency, partial fills, cancellations, stale quotes, spread widening, and nonlinear impact. Score robustness across liquid, thin, and shock regimes.
4. Use purged, embargoed walk-forward folds, frozen baselines, multiple seeds, and untouched event blocks. Compare risk and cost outcomes, not reward alone.

### Model hygiene

Prevent leakage, survivorship and look-ahead bias using point-in-time universes and first-known timestamps. Reconcile corporate actions and dividend conventions; reject stale options quotes. Control multiple testing, retain failed trials, calibrate out of sample, and monitor feature, probability, policy, regime, and execution-cost drift.

### MATLAB optimization

- Align sources with `timetable`, `retime`, and `synchronize`, preserving publication times and missingness; do not interpolate future information into earlier observations. [MathWorks](https://www.mathworks.com/help/matlab/ref/timetable.synchronize.html)
- Vectorize rolling features, preallocate episode buffers, and profile environment steps before parallelizing.
- Use independent walk-forward evaluations for parallel optimization, with recorded random streams and seeds; asynchronous completion can still affect reproducibility.
- Benchmark GPU benefit on realistic neural-network batch sizes. Keep environment, execution costs, constraints, and agent modules separate.
