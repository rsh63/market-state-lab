## 2. Order-level shadowing offers a passive-execution benchmark

![Illustrative passive execution, order-book, and quantitative-finance graphic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-2-quant.png)

*Credit: existing Market State Lab quantitative-finance category graphic; illustration.*

**Fact:** A September 16 preprint proposes Shadow-PPOV: follow observed third-party order additions at the same price and venue, then withdraw when the reference order ends. It evaluates the method against aggressive participation-of-volume execution using a full calendar year of CME ES market replay. [Maciejewski, arXiv:2609.18019](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18019)

**Inference:** A simple order-flow-based policy can provide a useful benchmark before adding complex fill-prediction or reinforcement-learning models.

**Uncertainty:** Replay is not live trading. Queue priority, order identifiers, cancellation latency, missed fills and counterfactual market impact limit transferability.

**Why it matters:** Benchmark MATLAB agents against passive and aggressive policies using implementation shortfall, completion, adverse selection and realistic latency—not filled-order performance alone.

### DRL Improvements for the MATLAB System

1. Add session, quote age, cancellation intensity, estimated queue position, NQ–ES strength, oil, curve changes and policy-event state.
2. Enforce drawdown, concentration, turnover, liquidity and daily-loss constraints outside the reward; penalize downside, adverse selection, missed fills and implementation shortfall inside it.
3. Randomize cancellation latency, partial fills, disappearing depth, headline gaps and nonlinear impact. Do not reward non-executable fills.
4. Use purged, embargoed walk-forward evaluation with frozen passive/aggressive POV baselines, multiple seeds, capacity tests and untouched event-regime blocks.

### Model hygiene

Control leakage, survivorship and look-ahead bias, first-known timestamps, corporate actions, stale options quotes and multiple testing. Reconcile source discrepancies before training, retain rejected trials, calibrate out of sample and monitor feature, probability, policy, transaction-cost and regime drift.

### MATLAB optimization

- Align market and event streams using `timetable`, `retime` and `synchronize`, preserving first-availability timestamps. [MathWorks timetables](https://www.mathworks.com/help/matlab/timetables.html)
- Vectorize rolling features, preallocate buffers and profile order-book and environment hot paths before parallelizing.
- Parallelize independent purged-fold Bayesian searches with reproducible random streams; keep validation blocks isolated.
- Reserve GPUs for sufficiently large neural-network batches. Keep order-book, execution-cost, constraint and agent modules separate and benchmark end-to-end performance.

### High-value research question

**Does passive order-flow shadowing retain an execution advantage over aggressive POV in ES across calm versus headline-driven sessions after queue priority, cancellation latency and missed-fill costs?**

It tests whether a simple, inspectable execution baseline survives the frictions that often disappear from backtests; transfer to ETF venues would require separate validation.
