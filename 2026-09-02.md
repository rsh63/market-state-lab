## 1. Anthropic splits its new frontier model between broad and restricted access

## 4. U.S. federal debt crosses $40 trillion as financing costs rise

![Abstract U.S. fiscal, Treasury, and Federal Reserve graphic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-4-fed.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab economics graphic.*

**Fact:** Reuters reported that gross U.S. federal debt exceeded $40 trillion. It cited a Congressional Budget Office estimate that tax and immigration legislation enacted in the current presidential term would add approximately $4.7 trillion to debt, while elevated yields increase financing costs. [Reuters, September 2](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-pledged-fiscal-restraint-instead-debt-tops-40-trillion-borrowing-costs-2026-09-02/)

**Inference:** Fiscal supply and term-premium risk can keep long yields elevated even when the expected policy-rate path is unchanged.

**Uncertainty:** Long-run debt projections and causal attribution are sensitive to growth, inflation, legislation, spending, and interest rates. The debt milestone does not imply an immediate Federal Reserve action.

**Why it matters:** Model the policy rate, Treasury supply, term premium, energy-driven inflation, and market-functioning interventions separately rather than collapsing them into one yield feature.

### Model hygiene

Control leakage, survivorship and look-ahead bias, first-known timestamps, corporate actions, stale options quotes, crossed markets, and multiple testing. Calibrate out of sample, retain rejected trials, and monitor feature, probability, policy, transaction-cost, and regime drift.

### MATLAB optimization

- Align market, factor, event, and contract data with `timetable`, `retime`, and `synchronize`.
- Vectorize rolling features, preallocate buffers, and profile environment steps and memory transfers before parallelizing.
- Run parallel `bayesopt` only across independent purged folds, using reproducible random streams and multiple policy seeds.
- Reserve GPUs for sufficiently large neural-network batches; keep factor-basis, execution-cost, risk-constraint, environment, and agent modules separate.
