## 2. CFTC penalizes trading on advance presidential-speech information

![Abstract quantitative-finance and event-market graphic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-2-quant.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab quantitative-finance graphic.*

**Fact:** The CFTC said former White House aide Gabriel Perez used advance access to presidential speeches to trade Kalshi mention-market contracts between December 2025 and February 2026. The settlement requires $107,539.02 in disgorgement, a $65,000 civil penalty, and a three-year trading ban; the agency said cooperation reduced the penalty. [Reuters, August 29](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/cftc-fines-former-white-house-aide-172000-trading-trump-speech-information-2026-08-29/)

**Inference:** Event-contract surveillance must combine market data with information-access provenance; price-only anomaly detection is insufficient.

**Uncertainty:** One enforcement action does not establish the incidence of similar conduct across prediction markets or traditional derivatives.

**Why it matters:** Add restricted-event lists, access logs, feature provenance, delayed-data controls, and human review around government speeches and other potentially material nonpublic information.

## 4. Warsh says inflation progress is not yet sufficient

[Federal Reserve speech](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm), [Reuters market reaction](https://www.reuters.com/business/view-rate-hike-expectations-rise-warsh-speech-jackson-hole-2026-08-28/)

**Why it matters:** Preserve separate hold, conditional-hike, and communication-shock regimes. Model expected policy rates, term premium, Treasury supply, and market-functioning support as distinct drivers.

## 5. Pasqal's Nasdaq debut highlights quantum-computing optimism—and execution risk

![Abstract technology-business and advanced-computing graphic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/story-5-business.png)

*Credit: original Market State Lab technology/business graphic.*

**Fact:** French quantum-computer maker Pasqal rose as much as 73% in its August 28 Nasdaq debut before paring the gain to about 40%. Its merger with Bleichroeder Acquisition Corp II valued the transaction at roughly $2 billion and supplied about $360 million in cash. Reuters reported 2025 revenue of €16.5 million and seven deployed machines. [Reuters, August 28](https://www.reuters.com/business/frances-quantum-computer-maker-pasqal-surges-nasdaq-debut-2026-08-28/)

**Inference:** Public-market enthusiasm is assigning option value to quantum hardware well before mature revenue and application economics are established.

**Uncertainty:** Quantum error rates remain a central technical obstacle; commercial timelines, dilution, margins, utilization, and a repeatable high-value application remain uncertain.

**Why it matters:** Evaluate quantum exposure using hardware reliability, workload advantage, utilization, customer concentration, revenue quality, cash runway, and dilution—not debut momentum alone.
