## 2. Options-exit research puts supervised learning before RL

![Three-stage options-exit research workflow: historical training labels, supervised MLP initialization, and risk-aware RL fine-tuning](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a0/c4/a0c47efd-8ef4-418e-9889-d29466ec5731/content/images/2026/10/2026-10-12-story-2-options-exits.png)

*Graphic: Market State Lab. Conceptual method diagram based on Wang, Ślepaczuk and Sakowski, October 9.*

**Fact:** An October 9 paper initializes SPX short-straddle exit policies with a supervised MLP, then fine-tunes using Actor–Critic. Authors report modest average improvement over the MLP; the equal-notional episode-level increment is not statistically significant. [Paper and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.13126v1)

**Inference:** A strong supervised baseline may contribute more than additional RL complexity.

**Uncertainty:** Results are not independently replicated; licensed data, synthetic continuation states and missing severe-crisis regimes limit generalization.

**Why it matters:** Compare MLP-only, initialized RL and fixed exits on identical purged episodes. Keep future-derived training labels entirely outside test inputs. This does not establish safety for uncovered options selling.

### Execution implications

- Separate premarket, the 8:30 a.m. CT opening auction, first half-hour, and closing auction. Use limits and participation caps tied to fresh depth; monitor realized slippage and cancellation rates.
- Treat the equity-open/bond-holiday session as a distinct state. Check actual hedge-venue hours and basis; stale cash Treasury marks can mislead cross-asset models.
- Rebuild SPY/QQQ option surfaces from synchronized, executable two-sided quotes. Reject stale, crossed, zero-bid and mismatched-leg observations. Avoid treating last trades as achievable combo fills.
- Dealer gamma direction and strike-level positioning are unverified. Tag each option's actual expiry/settlement; do not infer hedging flows from open interest alone. Isolate 0DTE tests from longer-dated exits.
- Map Wednesday–Friday inflation windows explicitly; stop using pre-release spread and impact estimates when observed conditions change.

**Watchlist:** SPY, QQQ, RSP, IWM, TLT, VIX, MTUM, VLUE, QUAL, IWF, SPLV, XLE, SMH, ES, NQ, ZN, VX.

### DRL Improvements for the MATLAB System

1. Keep a frozen supervised exit baseline; test initialized RL only as an incremental improvement under identical actions, episodes, fills and seeds.
2. Enforce drawdown, exposure, turnover, liquidity and loss constraints outside the reward. Include implementation shortfall and downside penalties without rewarding infeasible fills.
3. Stress gaps, missing quotes, partial fills, spread widening and hedge unavailability; distinguish observed data from synthetic continuation states.
4. Use purged, embargoed walk-forward evaluation, multiple seeds and untouched crisis/event blocks. Separate fixed exposure from dynamic sizing and report uncertainty around incremental gains.

### Model hygiene

Prevent leakage and look-ahead through first-known timestamps and label-horizon purging. Use point-in-time constituents to control survivorship bias; reconcile splits, dividends and corporate actions. Audit stale options quotes and multiple testing, retain failed trials, calibrate out of sample, and monitor feature, probability, policy, cost and regime drift.

### MATLAB optimization

- Align sessions and releases using `timetable`, `retime` and `synchronize`; preserve timezone and holiday metadata.
- Preallocate episode buffers and vectorize rolling features; profile environment steps before parallelizing.
- Run parallel `bayesopt` across independent purged folds with reproducible worker random streams; prevent nested oversubscription.
- Use GPUs only when batch size justifies transfer overhead; keep data, environment, execution and risk-constraint modules separate.

### High-value quant research question

**Does supervised initialization improve out-of-sample SPY/QQQ options-exit decisions beyond a frozen MLP baseline after identical exposure, quote-freshness filters and realistic costs, especially on equity-open/bond-holiday sessions and inflation-release days?**

This tests whether the incremental learning benefit survives execution friction and regime changes, rather than merely different position sizing.
