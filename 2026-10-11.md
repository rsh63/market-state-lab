## 2. Market-impact preprint challenges simplistic execution costs

![Two schematic curves show impact increasing with participation rate and remaining impact declining after execution; arbitrary units, not calibrated market data.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsh63/market-state-lab/main/2026-10-11-story-2-market-impact.png)

*Graphic: Market State Lab schematic, informed by Bonart’s October 8 preprint. Curves are illustrative, not reproduced research results or calibrated SPY/QQQ estimates.*

**Fact:** Julius F. Bonart's October 8 preprint models how arbitrageurs remove predictable returns, producing participation-dependent impact and impact decay under specified information and stationarity assumptions. [“Diffusive Market Impact: A Consistent Microfoundation,” arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11691)

**Inference:** Fixed-basis-point costs may misrank strategies that trade at different rates or repeatedly consume liquidity.

**Uncertainty:** This is theoretical, unreviewed research—not a calibrated SPY/QQQ execution model or an empirical performance guarantee.

**Why it matters:** Compare linear, square-root, and transient-impact models against actual fills; stress-test decay and participation assumptions before using them in DRL rewards.

### DRL Improvements for the MATLAB System

1. Add participation rate, spread, depth, quote age, concentration, session, and holiday states; allow a valid no-trade action.
2. Enforce drawdown, exposure, turnover, and liquidity constraints outside the reward; penalize implementation shortfall and non-executable fills inside it.
3. Randomize impact curvature, decay, latency, partial fills, opening gaps, and depth withdrawal to reduce dependence on a single simulator.
4. Use purged, embargoed walk-forward evaluation, nested tuning, frozen baselines, multiple seeds, and untouched event/holiday blocks.

### Model hygiene

Control leakage, survivorship and look-ahead bias, first-known timestamps, corporate actions, stale options quotes, and multiple testing. Keep survey release dates separate from reference periods; handle SCF imputation and weights correctly. Calibrate out of sample and monitor feature, probability, policy, regime, and transaction-cost drift.

### MATLAB optimization

- Align market sessions and release timestamps with `timetable`, `retime`, and `synchronize`; avoid filling closed sessions with synthetic tradable quotes.
- Vectorize rolling features, preallocate episode buffers, and profile environment steps before parallelizing.
- Use parallel `bayesopt` across independent purged folds with reproducible random streams.
- Use GPUs only for sufficiently large neural-network batches; separate environment, execution-cost, constraint, and agent modules.
